Problem

During urban floods, a dispatcher must decide which group goes to which shelter — while roads close and shelters fill. A route that looks safe now can be unsafe by the time people arrive.

What Sentinel does

  1. Suggests dispatches from live sensor and forecast data.
  2. Certifies each one against hundreds of possible futures, at the moment people would arrive.
  3. Blocks anything it cannot prove safe; the operator stays in control.
  4. Records a certificate that can be replayed to prove what happened.

Method

Real Fort Kochi road network (OpenStreetMap) · stochastic flood simulator · search planners vs reinforcement learning · retrieval over NDMA guidelines · statistical certification · hardware-in-the-loop sensors.
