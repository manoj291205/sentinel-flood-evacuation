Rules

  • Honesty banner on every operational page: real road topology, simulated conditions, not operationally validated.
  • Flat surfaces, 1–2 px rules, no gradients, no glass, no drop shadows.
  • Numbers in Plex Mono with tabular figures.
  • Touch targets ≥ 44 px; every control reachable by keyboard; WCAG AA contrast in both themes.
  • Every suggestion shows “why” in plain language next to the certified bound.
  • Never show a metric that is not loaded from a frozen result file; show “—” and the reason instead.
  • Stale data (no update for 5 s) gets a dashed outline and an age label.
  • Map: grid, north arrow, 100 m scale bar, dashed zone decals, ID plates that invert when selected.
