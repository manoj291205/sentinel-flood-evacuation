# Sentinel-Agent V3 — Retrieval Benchmark Specification

## Frozen status and versions

This document freezes `v3-retrieval-benchmark-spec-v1` before any human labels or
official retrieval results exist. Phase 13A creates machine proposals and review pools
under `v3-retrieval-benchmark-candidate-v1`. A future, completed human-reviewed set may
be frozen in Phase 13B as `v3-retrieval-benchmark-gold-v1`; that version does not yet
exist.

The evaluated inputs remain frozen:

- corpus: `v3-authoritative-corpus-v1`
- fingerprint: `a4877d0f837a07311e47bd1c3ca35cb4c368c533012b631ed67cbc8ff3aeb753`
- retrieval configuration: `v3-retrieval-v1`
- methods: `dense-minilm-v1`, `bm25-okapi-v1`, `hybrid-rrf-v1`

No Phase 12 parameter, chunk, document, or model may be changed after inspecting the
candidate pools or human labels. A necessary change requires a formal amendment and a
complete benchmark rerun. No reranker is part of this study.

## Human-ground-truth rule

Machine generation may propose questions, seed passages, explanations, and preliminary
grades. Every such judgment remains explicitly `MACHINE_PROPOSED`. It is never gold
truth. Only a real human may set question status to `ACCEPT`, `EDIT`, or `REJECT`, and
only a real human may assign passage grades. Human fields begin blank/PENDING.

No retrieval-quality result may be calculated from machine-proposed grades. Phase 13B
must refuse to run unless review completeness checks pass.

## Size, balance, and question selection

The generator creates at least 60 source-derived candidates so a human can reject weak
items. The final target is exactly 45 human-accepted/edited, answerable questions:

| Primary topic | Final target |
| --- | ---: |
| Flood risk / flood management | 9 |
| Evacuation / movement planning | 9 |
| Temporary shelters / relief camps | 9 |
| Vulnerable groups / accessibility | 9 |
| Logistics / transport / coordination | 9 |

The final set also targets 15 questions in each query style:

- `LEXICAL`: important official terminology appears in supporting evidence, without a
  long answer quotation being copied into the question.
- `SEMANTIC_PARAPHRASE`: wording materially differs from the supporting passage.
- `OPERATIONAL_TASK`: wording resembles a disaster-response decision maker's practical
  evidence need.

The 60-candidate packet uses 12 per topic and 20 per style, crossed evenly at four of
each style per topic. Human rejection may disturb the candidate balance; Phase 13B must
enforce the exact final topic totals and target style totals or stop for human-guided
replacement before running metrics.

Questions are generated by inspecting canonical source chunks, not by writing from a
retriever's preferred output. Every candidate must record source ID, filename, physical
PDF page, stable chunk ID, a verbatim supporting excerpt, and a machine explanation.
Queries must be clear without knowing the PDF and must not include document titles or
page numbers unnecessarily.

Reject candidates that are copied long quotations, contain their answer, ask trivial
metadata/yes-no questions, depend on invented policy facts, are ambiguous, or lack a
supporting canonical passage. Synthetic simulator thresholds, capacities, travel times,
or probabilities are forbidden unless the source explicitly states them. In particular,
the simulator's `0.70` threshold is not NDMA policy.

Each canonical document must support at least eight final accepted questions. At least
eight final questions should naturally have relevant evidence in two or more documents;
multi-source relevance is human-judged and is never inferred merely because multiple
sources appear in a retrieval pool.

## Page and evidence convention

`page` always means the existing one-based physical PDF page index used by Phase 11
chunks. It is not necessarily the page number printed inside the publication. Stable
benchmark references use that physical PDF page. Every review passage preserves original
chunk text so the reviewer can trace question → chunk → PDF → page → authoritative text.

## Frozen candidate pooling and reduction

For each already-written question, retrieve frozen top 10 from Dense, BM25, and Hybrid.
Union by stable `chunk_id`, never by text, filename, page, or rank. Force the
source-derived seed chunk into the union even if no retriever returns it.

Before labels, apply this deterministic reduction:

1. retain the seed;
2. mark passages appearing in at least two retriever lists as eligible;
3. mark each retriever's top five as eligible regardless of overlap;
4. deduplicate by chunk ID and verify source/page/text against the canonical catalog;
5. order the seed first, then best pooled rank ascending, then chunk ID ascending;
6. cap the packet at 18 passages per question.

This ordering preserves all three top-five lists because their union plus one seed is at
most 16; lower-ranked cross-method overlaps fill any remaining slots. No machine
relevance score removes passages after labels are observed.

> Relevance judgments are based on a pooled candidate set from the three evaluated
> retrieval systems plus source-derived seed evidence. Relevant chunks outside the pool
> may exist.

This is pooled—not exhaustive corpus-wide—annotation.

## Human question review

For every candidate, the reviewer decides:

1. Is it clear to a normal reader?
2. Is it answerable from the shown official seed evidence?
3. Is it non-trivial rather than a copied heading/answer?
4. Does it make sense without prior knowledge of the PDF?

Use `ACCEPT` if it is ready, `EDIT` plus `human_edited_question` if wording needs repair,
or `REJECT` if invalid/ambiguous. The reviewer need not provide personally identifying
information; `reviewer_role=project_owner` is sufficient if provenance is desired.

## Human passage relevance grades

For every pooled passage of each retained question, answer: “Would I use this passage as
evidence to answer this exact question?”

- `2 — DIRECTLY RELEVANT`: directly answers or materially supports the question and
  could reasonably be cited.
- `1 — PARTIALLY RELEVANT`: useful related context but insufficient to answer the main
  question alone.
- `0 — NOT RELEVANT`: does not materially help answer the question.

The source-derived seed is not automatically grade 2. Its machine suggestion must be
reviewed like every other passage. Same-topic text is not automatically directly
relevant.

Phase 13B readiness requires exactly 45 `ACCEPT`/`EDIT` questions, a completed human
grade for every pooled passage belonging to each retained question, and at least one
human grade-2 passage per retained question. Missing statuses or grades fail loudly; they
are never inferred.

Ambiguous questions are edited or rejected rather than assigned convenient evidence.
Multiple grade-2 passages—and grade-2 passages from multiple documents—are retained and
counted explicitly; no single-answer assumption is made. Questions that become
unanswerable, duplicate another accepted question, require facts outside the corpus, or
cannot be judged from the pool are excluded before evaluation. If fewer than 45 survive,
work stops for human-directed pre-evaluation replacement rather than generating items in
response to method performance.

## Frozen metrics and comparisons

The primary metric is **DirectHit@5**. A question scores one/1 when at least one human
grade-2 chunk appears in a retriever's first five results and 0 otherwise. The benchmark
score is the mean over exactly 45 retained questions. Grade 2 defines direct gold
evidence; grade 1 contributes only to graded ranking metrics.

Secondary metrics are:

- `DirectHit@1`, `DirectHit@3`, `DirectHit@5`: whether at least one grade-2 chunk appears
  within the stated cutoff;
- `MRR@5`: reciprocal rank of the first grade-2 chunk within the first five, or zero;
- `nDCG@5`: standard discounted cumulative gain using gain `2^grade - 1`, divided by the
  ideal ordering of pooled grades at cutoff five;
- `pooled Recall@5`: number of human grade-2 pooled chunks retrieved in the first five,
  divided by all human grade-2 chunks in that question's annotated pool.

Recall is explicitly pooled recall because relevant chunks outside the judged pool may
exist. The principal comparison is Hybrid RRF versus Dense, the current baseline. BM25
versus Dense and Hybrid versus BM25 are secondary comparisons and cannot replace the
principal hypothesis after results are observed.

The paired uncertainty unit is the **question**, not a passage. Phase 13B will use 10,000
paired bootstrap resamples with fixed seed `131313` and report 95% percentile confidence
intervals. Each draw samples 45 question indices with replacement and applies the same
indices to both compared methods.

The frozen interpretation is `HYBRID_SUPPORTED` only when all three conditions hold:

1. Hybrid mean DirectHit@5 minus Dense mean DirectHit@5 is at least `0.05`;
2. the paired 95% bootstrap CI lower bound for that difference is strictly greater than
   zero;
3. Hybrid nDCG@5 is at least Dense nDCG@5 minus `0.02`.

Otherwise the retrieval result is `HYBRID_NOT_SUPPORTED`. This claim concerns retrieval
only and cannot alter the frozen Phase 10 decision-policy result.

## Single-reviewer consistency audit

After the first human pass identifies the accepted set, fixed RNG seed `131313` selects
10 accepted question IDs for a second pass. Second-pass grades begin blank and are
entered separately. This is a single-reviewer consistency audit, not independent
inter-rater reliability. If a second real reviewer later participates, their labels must
be stored separately and described honestly.

## Frozen metrics for Phase 13B

Grade 2 is direct gold relevance. Grade 1 contributes only to graded metrics.

Primary metric: `DirectHit@5`, equal to one when at least one human grade-2 chunk appears
in a method's top five and zero otherwise; report its mean over the 45 questions.

Secondary metrics:

- `DirectHit@1`, `DirectHit@3`, `DirectHit@5`: presence of grade 2 by cutoff.
- `MRR@5`: reciprocal rank of the first grade-2 chunk within the top five, else zero.
- `nDCG@5`: graded relevance 0/1/2 with gain `2^grade - 1` and logarithmic discount,
  normalized by the ideal ordering of annotated-pool grades.
- `pooled Recall@5`: number of annotated grade-2 chunks retrieved in the top five divided
  by all grade-2 chunks in that question's reviewed pool.

Recall is explicitly pooled Recall@5 because relevant chunks outside the pool may exist.
The question—not passage—is the statistical unit.

## Frozen comparisons, bootstrap, and claim rule

The principal comparison is Hybrid versus Dense on DirectHit@5. BM25 versus Dense and
Hybrid versus BM25 are secondary reports. This ordering cannot change after results.

Use 10,000 paired bootstrap resamples of the 45 questions with replacement, fixed seed
`131313`, and a 95% percentile interval for method differences. Chunks are never treated
as independent sampling units.

Return `HYBRID_SUPPORTED` only if all conditions hold:

1. Hybrid DirectHit@5 − Dense DirectHit@5 ≥ 0.05;
2. the paired 95% bootstrap interval's lower bound is greater than zero;
3. Hybrid nDCG@5 ≥ Dense nDCG@5 − 0.02.

Otherwise return `HYBRID_NOT_SUPPORTED`. This is solely a retrieval-study result and
cannot alter the frozen Phase 10 decision-policy result (`NO`).

## Phase 13A stopping point

Synthetic unit fixtures may verify metric arithmetic and bootstrap determinism. Actual
Dense/BM25/Hybrid benchmark scores must not be computed until human review is complete.
Phase 13A ends with status `WAITING FOR HUMAN REVIEW`; no
`final_benchmark_results.json` exists and no natural-language answer generation or
entailment evaluation is performed.
